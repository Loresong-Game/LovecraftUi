LovecraftUI Examples

Serve this folder over HTTP (ES modules do not load from file://).

Showcases

  • game-hud.html — the 1.0 flagship: a complete combat HUD, fought in a drowned hall of cyclopean masonry with the tide on its floor.
  • demo.html — the full interactive showcase: every widget, window, and system.
  • embed.html — the interface inside a real scrolling page, over a consumer's own canvas, with destroy/reboot.
  • settings.html — a real settings screen: tabs, keybinds with conflicts, apply/revert, persistence.
  • inventory.html — item grids and the stacking drag protocol (merge, swap, split, seal).
  • trees.html — lattices of fate: twenty-two rites in three bloodlines, read as tiers, as ages, and as an unbound chart.
  • rich-text.html — the markup sampler: styled runs, inline icons, a live editor, non-Latin torture strings.
  • juice.html — choreography and echoes: transition gallery, stagger playground, the sound map, notify routing.
  • binding.html — the developer's bench: a fake model driving the HUD both ways, a DevPanel, a dev console that writes the same store, the node inspector, layout persistence.
  • big-data.html — ten thousand things: a log firehose, a 5k-row sorting table, a focusable 10k list.
  • meters.html — meters & measures: a wall of live instruments on one fake ticker.
  • meta-screens.html — thresholds: title menu, saves, difficulty, pause, level select, credits — end to end.
  • tutorial.html — the guiding hand: a tour that walks you through binding a ward at a lamplit bench, reagents and all.
  • theme-gallery.html — many skins: the shipped presets at four couch-distance scales, raw token overrides layered on top, a token diff, and photo mode.
  • strategy-hud.html — the war table: a drowned coast you command — marquee the thralls, right-click to send them, hold the bell when the tide answers.
  • survival-hud.html — the long winter: a camp where the day actually turns and warmth drains faster after dark.
  • mud-hud.html — the speaking stones: clickable room prose in a pinned scrollback, a command line, the wounded body doll, typed ring/amulet fittings.
  • narrative.html — the whispering stage: a staged landfall, letterboxed dialogue on a typewriter, a timed choice that leaves a real obligation, and a sanity slider that makes the interface tremble.
  • action-hud.html — the lantern bearer: a quay at night, a weapon wheel whose hit area is the ring, a boss walking its phases, and landmarks that leave the frame and come back.
  • deckbuilder.html — the drawn hand: a real one-fight loop — cards dealt flying, intents that telegraph and resolve, a reward pick, and the run map climbing floor by floor.
  • shooter-hud.html — the iron sights: mouse-panned aim over a harbor night, shots that raycast from the reticle, a killfeed, and a capture point you hold.
  • social-hud.html — the guildhall: a rankable guild roster, channel-voiced guild chat, party unit frames with a ready check, and the ground wheel.
  • tycoon.html — the counting house: a profit line that survives losses, a five-thousand-row ledger that expands and sorts, and an advisor center you can jump from.
  • arcade.html — a real match-three: matches slide and lock before they clear, cascades multiply, against a moves budget and a clock, ending in the counting-number tally with its rank seal.
  • icon-browser.html — the Icon Vault: all 3,840 pack icons, searchable, with copy-ready ids — and register your own painters beside them.

Add ?debug=1 to any engine page for the overlay: layout bounds, hit chain, clip rects, and the perf HUD.
